Intelligence Pipeline

How CompassIQ Thinks

Every ticket passes through a structured ML/NLP pipeline — from raw text to suggested resolution.

01

Understand

NLP preprocessing: lowercasing, tokenization, stopword removal, lemmatization. The raw ticket text is cleaned and normalized.

02

Vectorize

TF-IDF transforms the cleaned text into a sparse numerical feature vector used by the trained classification models.

03

Classify

Two Logistic Regression models independently predict the ticket Category and Priority with confidence scores.

04

Route

The predicted category determines the appropriate department. The ticket is automatically routed without manual triage.

05

Retrieve

Sparse cosine similarity against the historical corpus retrieves the Top-3 most similar resolved tickets.

06

Assist

A suggested resolution is presented to the agent, derived from actual historical resolved cases. The agent reviews and acts.

Core Capabilities

ML-First Ticket Management

CompassIQ doesn't just store tickets — it analyzes, classifies, routes, and assists resolution.

AI Ticket Classification

Logistic Regression models trained on TF-IDF features automatically classify every ticket into the correct category.

Priority Prediction

A separate ML model predicts ticket priority (Low / Medium / High / Critical) with confidence scoring.

Intelligent Department Routing

Predicted category maps tickets to the right department queue — Technical, Billing, Account, General, or Fraud & Security.

Historical Ticket Retrieval

Sparse cosine similarity retrieves the top-3 most relevant resolved tickets from the historical corpus.

Suggested Resolution

Resolution text from similar historical tickets is surfaced for the agent — insertable into the reply composer with one click.

Ticket Lifecycle Tracking

Full lifecycle from submission through AI analysis, department assignment, agent response, resolution, and CSAT feedback.
